# Persona: Marcus — Senior Field Technician

**Role:** Field HVAC Technician, 11 years experience
**Device:** Samsung Galaxy A-series (3 years old), Android 12
**Connectivity:** Spotty LTE, often 1 bar at job sites

## A Day in Marcus's Life

6:30am — On the road to first site. Coffee in one hand, phone in the other.
7:00am — At site. Gloves on before getting out of the van.
7:00–12:00 — On-site work. Phone in pocket. No time for apps between tasks.
12:00pm — Lunch break. First time phone is out properly.
1:00–5:00pm — More sites. Check-ins between jobs, in the van.
5:30pm — Last check-out. Done for the day.

## What Marcus Needs from the App

- Fast: check-in must complete in under 10 seconds
- Glove-friendly: large tap targets, no swipe gestures
- Offline: must work when there's no signal
- Clear confirmation: did it work? Yes or no. Not ambiguous.

## What Makes Marcus Close the App

- Any loading spinner over 3 seconds
- Login timeouts
- Multi-step flows when one step would do
- Pop-ups while he's in the middle of a job
