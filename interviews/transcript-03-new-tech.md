# Interview — Field Technician (New)
**Subject:** Devon, Field Tech, 4 months
**Context:** Still in onboarding, learning the systems

---

"They showed me the check-in app during training. I use it every time because I'm new and I don't want to get in trouble. But honestly it's confusing. Sometimes I'm not sure if I checked in or checked out. The screen goes back to the same place either way. And I once checked in to the wrong site because the search results looked similar and I was in a hurry. My supervisor figured it out but it was embarrassing."

**Pain points identified:**
- Check-in vs check-out ambiguity (same confirmation screen)
- Search result confusion for similar-named sites
- No error recovery for wrong-site check-in
