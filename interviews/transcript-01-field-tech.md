# Interview — Field Technician (Senior)
**Subject:** Marcus, Field Tech, 11 years
**Context:** Installs and maintains HVAC systems at commercial buildings

---

"I'm on the road by 6:30. First job site usually by 7. My phone is in my pocket — I can't be fumbling with it when I've got gloves on and I'm carrying equipment. The check-in thing they gave us last quarter? I have to take my gloves off, unlock the phone, find the app, log in every time because it logs me out — why does it log me out? — and then I have to type the site code. I can't even read the screen in direct sunlight. Half the time I just skip it and write it on paper."

**Pain points identified:**
- Requires gloves off
- Session timeout forcing re-login
- Site code manual entry
- Screen unreadable in sunlight
- No confirmation feedback (did it work?)
